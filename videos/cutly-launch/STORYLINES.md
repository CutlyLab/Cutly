# Cutly launch film storylines

## Recommended — The Evidence Chain

**Device / form:** result first, carried backward through an object-to-object
chain.

The first frame is a finished `artifact.mp4` with a verified evidence receipt.
The receipt becomes the carrier that pulls us backward through Cutly's typed
transaction, semantic plan, host-owned execution manifest, native pipeline,
and finally back to the verified artifact. It feels controlled, exact, and
physical rather than abstractly futuristic.

**First two seconds:** a full-width film strip locks into place; a cyan
`VERIFIED` bar crosses it and exposes the artifact/evidence binding.

**Beats:** verified result → browser dependency rejected → Cutly reveal → typed
transaction → frozen execution chain → native MP4 and evidence → open-source
end card.

**Look:** alternating bone-white editorial paper, anodized-black workstation,
and one cyan process colour; large industrial typography and printed code.

**Sound:** restrained 112 BPM mechanical-electronic bed; exact clicks on
verification and transaction commit, one low impact on the native output.

## Alternative — One Continuous Render Line

**Device / form:** the object is the explanation, filmed as one continuous
horizontal take. A single exact-time line travels through Document, Plan,
Manifest, Scheduler, Native Render, and Evidence while the camera follows.
The film would be calmer and more architectural, with less kinetic type.

## Alternative — The Webpage Breaks Apart

**Device / form:** life before and after with match cuts. A browser page
fragments into unstable layers, then those fragments lock into Cutly's typed
document and deterministic render pipeline. This is the boldest version, but
it risks putting too much attention on the browser-first contrast instead of
the product's own evidence model.
