# Code of Conduct

Cutly is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience or level of participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical claims with evidence.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, attribution and project security boundaries.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users, compatibility or safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
disruption and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict or
permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and protect the
reporter's confidentiality.

Report platform abuse through GitHub's reporting tools. To contact the Cutly
maintainers privately when no other private channel is available, use the
[private security-advisory form](https://github.com/CutlyLab/Cutly/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `Conduct report`. Do not post personal information in
a public issue.

This policy applies to repository activity, project communication and public
representation of the Cutly project.
